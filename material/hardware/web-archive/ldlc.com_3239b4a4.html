Overview

The dome camera for homes, residences and small to medium-sized businesses


With simple installation features and a high performance-to-cost ratio, the Dahua Entry IPC-HDBW1230DE-SW series network camera is a perfect model for securing your small to medium-sized home or residence. It is also well suited to small retail shops and other small and medium-sized businesses.With an advanced scene-adaptive bitrate control algorithm, Dahua smart encoding technology achieves higher encoding efficiency than H.265 and H.264. H.265+ and H.264+ compression delivers high quality video and reduces the cost of storage and transmission. When moving objects appear in the surveillance image, Dahua Motion detection technology triggers an alarm or recordings. The Dahua day/night function provides a colour image when the lighting is adequate. When lighting is insufficient, the camera switches to night mode and provides a white and black image. It is waterproof and dust resistant, It complies with IP67 and IK10 standards so it is protected against vandalism.

Features :

  • 1/3" 2 Megapixel CMOS sensor
  • Resolution (1920 x 1080 pixels)
  • Focal length: 2.8 mm Horizontal: 100°; Vertical: 53°; Diagonal: 120° Compression
  • Compression H.265, H.265+, H.264+, H.264
  • Night vision: IR up to 30 metres
  • Power supply
  • Connectivity: Fast Ethernet / Wi-Fi
  • microSD card slot (SDHC/SDXC up to 256 GB)
  • Certification: IP67, IK10
  • Dimensions: Ø 110.7 x 88.7 mm
  • Weight: Approx. 414 g
