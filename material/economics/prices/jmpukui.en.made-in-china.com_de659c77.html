Product Description

Water Vending Machine

Features:

1. Waterproof carbon steel cabinet and coat outdoor powder, the front door open design, safety and health, durable and easy installation.
2. Advanced RO purification techniques, seven levels effective water treatment and two sterilization of UV and ozone.
3. Airtight filling & high concentrations of ozone sterilization, eliminate the secondary pollution completely.
4.24 hour uninterrupted UV sterilization, guarantee the water quality.
5. There is one 200lilter storage water tank inside the machine.
6. LCD advertisement player can be add (option )
7. Machines have CE certificate.

Water purification system:
First stage: PP filter
Second stage: UDF bulk carbon filter
Third stage: CTO sinter carbon filter
Fourth stage: PPfilter
Fifth stage: RO filter
Sixth stage: Post activated carbon filter
Seventh stage: Mineralization filter
Eighth stage: UV sterilization
Ninth stage: Ozone sterilization

Vending system:
1. auto vending system work in coin, paper money, IC card, give change (option )
2. MDB protocol system is available
3. GSM remoter is available

The machine supports coin, paper money, and IC card payments. It also offers a change-giving option and is compatible with the MDB protocol.
What is the water purification process?
It uses a nine-stage system including PP, carbon, RO filters, followed by UV and ozone sterilization to ensure safe, high-quality water.
Can the machine size and capacity be customized?
Yes, full customization is available. Standard dimensions are 800x680x1900mm, and water capacity ranges from 400 to 3000 GPD.

The minimum order quantity is 1 piece.

Is the machine suitable for outdoor installation?
Yes, it features a waterproof carbon steel cabinet with outdoor powder coating for durability in various environments.

We accept LC, T/T, and D/P as terms of payment.

An LCD advertisement player is available as an optional feature.
